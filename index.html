NYC Farmers Markets

NYC farmers markets are open-air markets where local farmers and food producers
sell fresh products directly to customers. Many markets are part of the GrowNYC Greenmarket
program, which supports regional farms and provides New Yorkers with fresh,
locally sourced food. Farmers markets also offer a chance to meet the people
who grow and produce the food while supporting local agriculture.
